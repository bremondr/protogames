The tale in three chapters

Everything a board needs, and nothing it doesn't.

I

Draw

Brush, fill and line across forests, seas and deserts. Shape the board like a circle, an endless canvas, or a real country.

II

Share

A link carries the whole board in its address, so friends open their own copy. Save a file, or export a picture.

III

Play

Print it at the tile size you choose, over several pages or one big sheet, and meet around the table.
