Everything a board needs, nothing it doesn't

A drawing tool built for prototyping: quick to paint, easy to change, ready to print.

Three grids, many shapes

Hexagon, square or triangle tiles on a rectangle, hexagon, triangle or circle board, or an endless canvas you pan across.

Country boards

Cut the board to the outline of a real country or a region such as Scandinavia or the Balkans, with the borders highlighted.

Themed palettes

Terrain tones like forest, water and desert, or a cosmic palette for space games, with textured tiles and objects.

Fast tools

Brush, fill and line, an eraser, undo and redo, pan and zoom, and keyboard shortcuts for all of it.

Print at real size

A PDF with tiles at the size you choose, split over A4, Letter or A3 pages with assembly hints, or on one large sheet with crop marks.

Share and playtest

A share link carries the whole board in the address. Playtest mode locks editing so you can play on it.

From idea to table in four steps

No install, no sign-up, no build step.

  1. Pick a boardChoose a grid, a shape and a size.
  2. Paint itTerrain, objects, fills and lines.
  3. Share or saveA link, a project file, PNG or SVG.
  4. Print and playA true-size PDF for the table.
